How it works 原理
  1. Generate Gaussian white noise on the grid. 在網格上產生高斯白雜訊。
  2. Smooth it with a Gaussian filter of width σ. σ = 0 means no smoothing. 用寬度 σ 的高斯濾波平滑；σ = 0 代表不平滑。
  3. The top-k cells become mines (your first click and its neighbours are kept safe). 數值最高的前 k 格變成雷（第一下點擊處與鄰居保證安全）。
  4. Moran's I is computed with binary queen-contiguity weights, the same neighbourhood the hint numbers use. Moran's I 用二元 queen 權重計算，跟提示數字用的鄰居定義一樣。

Tobler's First Law: near things are more related than distant things. When mines cluster, one mine tells you a lot about its neighbours.
Tobler 地理學第一定律：越近的東西越相關。雷聚集時，看到一顆雷就知道附近很危險。
